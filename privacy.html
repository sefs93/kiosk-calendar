Privacy policy

Kiosk Calendar is a personal, single-user desk display. This policy covers what it does with Google data.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it requests read-only access to calendar events (calendar.events.readonly). It cannot create, change or delete anything, and it does not access email, files, contacts or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Event times and titles are used only to draw the display on the owner's tablet: the agenda, the next meeting, reminders and daily meeting totals.

Storage and sharing

Events are held in memory on the tablet and refreshed from Google directly. They are not sent to any other server, not stored by the developer, not sold, and not shared with anyone. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Kiosk Calendar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
